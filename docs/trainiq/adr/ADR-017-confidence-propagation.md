# ADR-017 — Confidence Propagation

**Status:** Accepted (Phase 0B, Milestone A — Normalization Engine)
**Origin:** Following ADR-016, the Chief Architect observed that confidence should not originate only at the final recommendation — it should be produced by every layer and combined, not invented late.

## Context

A single end-of-pipeline "confidence score" cannot explain *why* a recommendation is uncertain (e.g. "missing HR data for two workouts" vs. a vague "the AI is unsure").

## Decision

Every layer (Connector → Normalization → Knowledge Builder → Coach) produces output, metadata, and a confidence signal. The Coach inherits and combines confidence; it never invents its own.

## Consequences

- Directly realized in Epic 6 as `source_confidence`, computed from raw field completeness at the Normalization layer, independent of training load (a deliberate architectural separation confirmed during Epic 6 review).
- Sets the precedent later formalized as ADR-025 (Confidence Is Deterministic): if confidence must propagate from evidence, it cannot also be freely generated by an LLM at the end.
